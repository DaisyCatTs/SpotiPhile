# Design reset, 2026-10-08

Daisy rejected the first shell draft, including its cream/gold accent and overall
appearance. Its structural checks do not establish visual quality or approval.
The three first-generation Figma frames, `phase1-shell.html`, their captures,
`tokens.proposed.json` and their validation records are retained as rejected
history. Do not implement their palette or composition in the native client.

The reset requires primary-source comparative research, a new visual review,
theme and density verification, then native implementation of the reviewed
direction. The product is a desktop music workspace for repeated listening and
large-library browsing. Its first viewport must contain music and useful
controls, rather than descriptions of the design or roadmap.

Daisy selected Spotiphile as the product name and then instructed continuation
of Phase 1. This authorizes the replacement direction's native implementation.
The name decision does not authorize
renaming the package, repositories, credentials, profile directories or updater.
Dark, Light and OLED are separate base themes; user-selected accents are a
separate setting. Cream/gold is removed from the proposed default direction.
Winamp and MilkDrop remain first-class entry points and retain their own visual
behavior. No Spotify Lossless, bit-perfect or unsupported DSP claims belong in
the mockup.

The canonical master plan Task C requires approved Figma/tokens before broad
visual implementation. Daisy authorized continuation after the concrete replacement
review. Phase 1 remains in progress. New review artifacts are
design evidence, not the native executable or a completed Phase 1.

Daisy subsequently rejected the replacement prototype and native composition.
The name and theme requirements remain. See [the second reset review](PROFESSIONAL_DESIGN_REVIEW.md)
for specific defects and replacement gates. The earlier continuation instruction
does not make the rejected appearance approved. Broad native styling is paused
while a stronger full-screen proposal is prepared and inspected.
